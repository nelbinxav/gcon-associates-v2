Services

Everything a building needs before, during and after construction.

Design, approvals, cost and site quality, handled by the same team.

01

Architectural design

Concept to sanction drawings for residential buildings, hotels, malls, farm houses, water parks and banquets.

  • Landscaping and external development
  • As-built site measurement
02

Structural design

Structural engineering by senior engineers, supported by geotechnical advice from soil specialists.

  • Bar bending schedules (BBS)
  • Geotechnical engineering
03

Interior design

Offices, hospitals, hotels, restaurants, showrooms and homes, designed with the same cost discipline as the building around them.

04

Sanction and occupancy

We prepare the drawings, obtain building plan sanction from the competent authority and secure the occupancy certificate.

05

Quantity surveying and bill auditing

Cost management and independent checking of contractor bills.

  • Structure, finishing, interior and landscape works
  • Electrical and plumbing works
  • Measurement book (MB) checks
06

Surveying, PMC and quality audit

Land surveying, project management consultancy and supervision of construction material and workmanship.
